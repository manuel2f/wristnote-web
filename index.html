How it works

The watch is the button, the phone does the work

The watch never records audio. It starts and stops the recording on your paired phone and shows its state, so you get the phone's better microphone and battery.

Press Start

Open WristNote from the complication on your watch face. You can also use the Quick Settings tile or the in-app button.

The phone records

A visible notification with a Stop button stays on while recording. It stops by itself after 5 minutes.

Stop from anywhere

From the watch, the notification, the tile or the app. The watch vibrates to confirm.

Read your note

The note is transcribed and summarized on the phone, ready to search, edit or share.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the watch record the audio?
No. The watch is only the button and the status display. Audio is captured by your phone's microphone and stored only in the app's private storage on the phone.
Which phones and watches are supported?
Transcription needs a phone with Android's on-device speech recognition (AICore), such as recent Pixel phones. To record from the watch you need a paired Wear OS watch with WristNote installed on both devices. The watch is optional: you can also record from the Quick Settings tile or the app.
Which languages are transcribed?
Spanish for now.
Can it record with the phone locked?
Yes. Starting from the watch or the Quick Settings tile works with the phone in your pocket. While recording, Android shows the microphone indicator and an ongoing notification with a Stop button.
Is my data private?
Your recordings, transcriptions and summaries never leave your phone: there are no accounts, no own servers and no ads. The Google ML Kit libraries that run transcription may send Google technical usage metrics, which never include your audio or text. Details in the privacy policy.
Can I record other people?
You are responsible for informing and getting consent from the people you record when the law requires it.
